Minimum order

The minimum wholesale order is 10 lb per delivery. Wholesale coffee is packed in 5 lb bags. Tiers are reviewed every quarter based on your average weekly volume.

Delivery schedule

Partners in the Portland metro area receive free delivery on Tuesdays and Fridays. Orders must be placed by 2:00 pm the day before delivery. Partners outside the metro area receive orders by UPS Ground, billed at cost.

Payment terms

The first three orders are prepaid by credit card. After three on-time orders, partners can apply for Net 30 invoicing, subject to credit approval. Overdue invoices accrue a late fee of 1.5% per month.

Ordering

Orders are placed through the wholesale portal or by emailing your account manager. Standing weekly orders can be set up so you never run out.
