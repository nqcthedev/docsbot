Coffee Subscriptions

Fresh coffee on your schedule. Subscribers save 10% on every bag and get free shipping within the US.

How it works

  • Choose any coffee from our lineup, or pick Roaster's Choice for a different single origin every delivery.
  • Choose a delivery frequency: every 1, 2, 3 or 4 weeks.
  • Your card is charged on the roast date of each delivery, not when you sign up.

Skipping, pausing and cancelling

You can skip a delivery, pause your subscription for up to 3 months, or cancel at any time from the Subscriptions tab of your account page. There are no cancellation fees.

Changes must be made before 11:59 pm Pacific Time on the day before your next roast date. After that, the upcoming order is already in the roasting queue and will ship.

Gift subscriptions

Gift subscriptions are prepaid for 3, 6 or 12 months and do not renew automatically. The recipient receives an email with a message from you on the date you choose.

Shipping for subscribers

Subscription orders ship free with standard shipping anywhere in the US. Subscriptions to Canada pay the regular Canada flat rate described on our shipping page.
