About Northwind

Northwind started in 2014 with a secondhand 5-kilo roaster in a Portland garage.

Our story

Founders Maya Okafor and Daniel Reyes met while working as baristas. They began roasting for friends, then for a handful of neighborhood cafés. In 2019 we opened our roastery and café on SE Alder Street, where we now roast on a 15-kilo machine.

How we source

We buy most of our coffee directly from the same producers every harvest and pay well above commodity prices. Each bag lists the farm or washing station it came from.

Giving back

One percent of every online sale goes to a fund that supports agronomy training for the farming communities we buy from.
