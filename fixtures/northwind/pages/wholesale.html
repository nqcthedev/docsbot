Wholesale

We partner with cafés, restaurants, bakeries and offices across the Pacific Northwest.

Who we work with

Our wholesale partners range from neighborhood cafés serving a few hundred drinks a day to corporate offices with self-serve brewers. Every partner gets a dedicated account manager.

How to apply

Fill out the wholesale application with your business name, location and estimated weekly coffee volume. We review applications within 3 business days and send approved partners our Wholesale Program Guide.

Pricing, minimums and equipment

Wholesale pricing is tiered by weekly volume. Minimum order quantities, delivery schedules, payment terms and espresso equipment leasing are explained in the Wholesale Program Guide.

Training and support

New partners receive on-site barista training at onboarding, plus ongoing support from our coffee quality team.
