Pour-over Brew Guide

Our house recipe for a V60 or Kalita Wave, making one large mug.

What you need

  • 20 g of coffee, ground medium-fine (like table salt)
  • 320 g of water at 96 °C (205 °F) — a 1:16 ratio
  • A paper filter, rinsed with hot water

Steps

  1. Bloom: pour 50 g of water and wait 40 seconds while the coffee releases gas.
  2. Pour slowly in circles until the scale reads 200 g at 1:15.
  3. Finish pouring to 320 g by 2:00.
  4. The drawdown should finish between 3:00 and 3:30 total brew time.

Troubleshooting

If the coffee tastes sour or thin, grind finer. If it tastes bitter or the brew takes longer than 4 minutes, grind coarser.
