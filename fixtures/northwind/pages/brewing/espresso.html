Espresso Brew Guide

A starting point for Night Shift Espresso on a home or café machine.

Recipe

Dose18 g of finely ground coffee
Yield36 g of liquid espresso (a 1:2 ratio)
Time27–30 seconds from when you start the shot
Temperature93 °C (199 °F)

Resting the beans

Espresso tastes best when the beans have rested 7 to 21 days after the roast date. Very fresh coffee produces a lot of gas and makes shots run unevenly.

Dialing in

If the shot runs faster than 25 seconds, grind finer. If it runs slower than 32 seconds, grind coarser. Change only one variable at a time.
