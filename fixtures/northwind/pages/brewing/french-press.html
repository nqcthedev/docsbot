French Press Brew Guide

Rich, full-bodied coffee with almost no special equipment.

Recipe

  • 30 g of coffee, ground coarse (like sea salt)
  • 450 g of water just off the boil, about 94 °C (200 °F) — a 1:15 ratio

Steps

  1. Add the coffee, pour all the water and start a timer.
  2. At 4:00, stir the crust gently and skim off the foam.
  3. Press the plunger slowly and pour immediately so the coffee doesn't keep extracting.

Recommended coffees

Harbor House Blend and Sumatra Lintong are our favorites for French press.
