Cold Brew Guide

Smooth, low-acid concentrate that keeps in the fridge.

Recipe

  • 100 g of coffee, ground extra coarse
  • 500 g of cold filtered water — a 1:5 ratio for concentrate

Steps

  1. Combine the coffee and water in a jar and stir until all the grounds are wet.
  2. Cover and steep in the refrigerator for 18 hours.
  3. Strain through a paper filter.
  4. Dilute 1 part concentrate with 1 part water or milk and serve over ice.

Storage

Cold brew concentrate keeps for up to 10 days in a sealed container in the refrigerator.
