Shipping Information

We roast on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays and ship from our roastery in Portland, Oregon.

Order cutoff

Orders placed before 10:00 am Pacific Time on a roast day ship the same day. Orders placed after the cutoff, or on a non-roast day, ship on the next roast day.

United States

MethodDelivery timeCost
Standard (USPS Ground Advantage)2–5 business days$5.95, free on orders of $45 or more before tax
Expedited (UPS 2nd Day Air)2 business days$14.95

Expedited shipping is not available for PO boxes or APO/FPO addresses.

Canada

We ship to Canada for a flat rate of $16.00 USD per order, with delivery in 5–10 business days. Import duties and taxes may be collected on delivery and are the responsibility of the customer. The free-shipping threshold does not apply to Canadian orders.

Other countries

At the moment we only ship to the United States and Canada.

Local pickup

Choose "Pickup at the roastery" at checkout to collect your order for free at our café on SE Alder Street. Pickup orders are ready within 2 hours during café hours. See visiting the roastery.

Tracking and lost packages

You will receive a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships. If a package has not arrived within 14 days of its expected delivery date, contact us and we will send a replacement or a refund.
