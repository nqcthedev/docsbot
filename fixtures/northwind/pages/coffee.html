Freshness and roast dates

Every bag is printed with its roast date. Orders ship within 24 hours of roasting. We recommend brewing within four to six weeks of the roast date for the best flavor.

Limited releases

A few times a year we release micro-lots in small quantities. Limited releases are announced to subscribers first and are not eligible for subscription discounts.
