Visit the Roastery & Café

418 SE Alder Street, Portland, Oregon (a fictional address for this demo).

Café hours

Monday – Friday7:00 am – 4:00 pm
Saturday – Sunday8:00 am – 3:00 pm

We are closed on Thanksgiving Day, December 25 and January 1.

Public cupping

Join our public cupping every Saturday at 10:00 am. Tickets are $15 per person, include a 4 oz bag of coffee to take home, and are limited to 12 guests. Book online up to two weeks ahead.

Getting here

Free street parking is available on SE Alder and SE 12th Avenue, and there are bike racks out front. Well-behaved dogs are welcome on the patio.

Customer support

Our support team answers emails Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm Pacific Time, usually within one business day. Write to hello@northwind-roasters.example.
