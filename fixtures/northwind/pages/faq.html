Frequently Asked Questions

Is your packaging recyclable?

Our bags are certified home-compostable. The one exception is the small one-way degassing valve: peel it off and put it in the trash before composting the bag.

Are your coffees organic?

Our Sumatra Lintong and Swiss Water Decaf Peru are certified organic. Our other coffees are not certified, but we buy them from farms we visit and know well.

How is your decaf made?

Our decaf is decaffeinated with the Swiss Water Process, which uses only water, temperature and time — no chemical solvents. It is 99.9% caffeine-free.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes. Digital gift cards are available from $25 to $200 and never expire. They can be used online and at the roastery café.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Shop Pay. We do not accept PayPal at this time.

Can I change or cancel an order after placing it?

One-time orders can be changed or cancelled until they enter the roasting queue at 10:00 am Pacific Time on the next roast day. After that, the order is roasted and shipped and can be handled under our returns policy.

Do you sell flavored coffee?

No. We only sell unflavored coffee so the character of each origin comes through.
