Returns & Freshness Promise

We want you to love every cup. If something isn't right, we'll fix it.

Freshness Promise (opened coffee)

If you don't love a coffee, tell us within 30 days of delivery and we will send you a different coffee of equal value or refund the bag. The Freshness Promise applies once per customer per coffee, and you don't need to send the bag back.

Unopened coffee

Unopened bags can be returned for a full refund within 30 days of delivery. Return shipping is paid by the customer unless we made a mistake with your order.

Brewing equipment

Grinders, kettles and brewers can be returned within 30 days of delivery if unused and in their original packaging. Opened electric grinders are subject to a 15% restocking fee. Equipment that arrives defective is covered by the manufacturer's warranty, and we will help you file the claim.

Damaged orders

If your order arrives damaged, email us a photo within 7 days of delivery and we will ship a replacement at no cost.

Non-refundable items

Gift cards and prepaid gift subscriptions are non-refundable.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.
